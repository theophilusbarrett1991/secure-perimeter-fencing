Questions

Good to know

What is Heras fencing?

It's the standard temporary mesh fencing you see around building sites: galvanised steel panels that slot into weighted feet and clip together to make a secure fence line.

Do you put it up and take it down?

Yes. We deliver, install, dismantle and collect, so you don't have to lift a panel.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how much fencing you need, where the site is and how long you need it. Call or email and we'll give you a clear quote.

How long can I hire it for?

As long as the job needs. Tell us your dates when you get in touch, and let us know if they change.

What do you need from me for a quote?

Your location, how many panels you need (or a rough length in metres and we'll work it out), and your hire dates. A photo or sketch of the site helps too.
